# Welcome to ReVMSET

There was a time when everything about games felt magical.

Not just the stories or the pixel art or the thrill of discovery--but the music. Those tunes are still with us. Not always the big main themes everyone remembers, but the little ones that caught you off guard. A stage you only reached once. A menu you lingered in too long. Music that stuck in your brain and quietly shaped your childhood.

**ReVMSET** is about that music.

It's a place for sharing and revisiting the soundtracks that often get overlooked. The odd ones. The brilliant ones. The ones you might not have heard in twenty years, but your fingers still remember the button presses that went with them.

We're not chasing nostalgia just for nostalgia's sake. These tracks still hold up. Some are genuinely great compositions. Others are little time machines. All of them are worth hearing again—-and maybe playing on loop while you work (we won't tell).

There's no fixed schedule here. No algorithm to game. Just a growing collection of posts exploring why this music matters, how it was made, and what it still says to us now. If that sounds good, then you're in the right place.

Welcome to ReVMSET — Retro Videogame Music: Soundtracks Explored Thoughtfully.
